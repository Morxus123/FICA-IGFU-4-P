# FICA–IGF-U v4.0 Domain Catalog

| Domain | Example subfields | Primitive research objects |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | pure, applied, statistics, probability | expression, equation, set, function, proof |
| Computer & information sciences | algorithms, AI, software, data science | program, algorithm, graph, dataset, model |
| Physical sciences | physics, astronomy, optics | quantity, field, particle, state, measurement |
| Chemical sciences | chemistry, analytical, physical, materials | species, reaction, concentration, equilibrium |
| Earth & environmental | geology, climate, oceanography, ecology | location, layer, sample, climate state |
| Biological sciences | genetics, microbiology, zoology, evolution | cell, gene, population, trait, network |
| Engineering & technology | civil, mechanical, electrical, aerospace, nanotech | component, system, load, signal, control |
| Medical & health | medicine, clinical, public health, epidemiology | observation, biomarker, intervention, cohort |
| Agricultural & veterinary | agriculture, forestry, fisheries, veterinary | crop, animal, soil, trial, yield |
| Social sciences | psychology, economics, education, sociology, law | agent, survey, institution, network |
| Humanities & arts | history, archaeology, languages, philosophy, arts | text, artifact, document, concept, argument |

The catalog is intentionally broad and extensible. It is a computational ontology, not a claim that the listed primitive objects exhaust any field.
